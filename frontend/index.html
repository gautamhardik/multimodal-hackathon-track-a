      1. 01

        Routine data

        Demographics, symptoms, ECG, echocardiography and laboratory values from one clinic visit. No imaging of the arteries themselves.

      2. 02

        Four models

        One for overall coronary artery disease (XGBoost) and one per artery (random forests), each chosen by nested cross-validation.

      3. 03

        Calibration

        Platt scaling fitted on development data only, so a 70% estimate means roughly seven in ten similar patients had the narrowing.

      4. 04

        Explanation

        Exact SHAP values show which measurements pushed each estimate up or down, next to the patient's value and its normal range.
